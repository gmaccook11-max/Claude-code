RE-Mind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

RE-Mind helps students keep track of a daily checklist, school assignments and grades. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your information stays on your device, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Information you enter

Your name, goals, answers to the setup questions, checklists, assignments, grades and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and we cannot see them. Deleting the app, or using "Erase everything" in Settings, removes them.

School calendar links

If you connect a school calendar (for example from Schoology or Canvas), the app downloads that calendar to read your assignments. When a school site does not allow the app to download it directly, the request passes through RE-Mind's server, which fetches the calendar and passes it straight back to your device. The server does not store the link or the calendar.

Screenshots of grades

When you scan a screenshot of your grades, the text is read on your device. The image is never uploaded.

Reminders

In the iPhone app, reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS and are not sent to any server. In the web version of RE-Mind, reminders that arrive while the app is closed are stored on RE-Mind's server until they are sent: the reminder text (such as an assignment name) and the time it is due. Each reminder is deleted once it has been sent, and turning notifications off deletes all of them.

What we do not do

RE-Mind does not have user accounts, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

RE-Mind is made for students. Because it does not collect personal information on its servers, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on RE-Mind's App Store page.
